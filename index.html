Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Welcome to Tuation ("Tuation - Tutor & Batch Manager", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of tutors, instructors, and their students. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

Important Summary: Tuation is a 100% offline-first application. All data entered into Tuation is stored locally and securely on your own device. We do not host, store, or transmit your records to cloud servers.

1. Information We Collect

Because Tuation runs entirely offline on your local device database, we do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All records you input remain strictly under your control, including:

  • Student & Batch Records: Names, contact numbers, repeating weekly class day choices, and notes.
  • Schedules & Attendance: Lesson times, dates, and manually tracked tuition done/completion flags.
  • Fee Logs & Expense Data: Payment histories, custom categories, cash flows, and Rupee transactions.
  • Academic Performance: Homework assignments, due dates, test schedules, and student marks.

2. Data Storage & Security

All data is saved in a secure, encrypted local database (SQLite/Room) partitioned within your device's isolated storage directory. No student records are ever transmitted over the network.

  • Security PIN: The application includes an optional 4-digit PIN lock screen, which is validated locally on your device to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Backups: You have complete ownership of your backups. Exporting data to a CSV spreadsheet saves the file locally on your device storage, allowing you to manage or transfer files as needed.

3. Permissions Required

To function correctly, Tuation may request the following permissions on your mobile device:

  • Notifications: To send reminders about scheduled classes, homework, or fee collections (Android 13+).
  • Storage (Read/Write): Necessary only when you choose to export your records to a CSV spreadsheet or import a previous backup file.

4. Third-Party Services & Trackers

Tuation does not use any third-party SDKs, marketing trackers, telemetry platforms, or advertising networks. Your in-app behavior remains completely private.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our offline local database features. We recommend checking this page periodically. Any updates are effective immediately upon app installation.
